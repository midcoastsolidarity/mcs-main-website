Data Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

This page explains what our forms collect, who holds that data, and how to get it deleted. We don't have any lawyers, so this isn't a legal privacy policy. If something looks wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

Our website

Our website doesn't run any JavaScript and doesn't set any cookies. GitHub hosts it, so GitHub can see which pages you load here.

Our forms

Our two signup forms are hosted by JotForm, a separate company. What you type into them goes to JotForm, not to this website.

The Email List and Interest Form asks for:

  • Your name, pronouns, town, and the best way to reach you
  • A phone number and Signal username, only if you want the virtual book club
  • Which activities and book club meetings interest you
  • Skills you can share, a project you need help with, and how you heard about us

The Membership Interest Form asks for:

  • Your name and email address
  • Whether you want to become a member, and whether you have time for a vetting interview
  • Which of our events you have attended or plan to attend

Who holds your data

JotForm processes and stores your answers for us as a third-party service. JotForm stores form responses in its [REGION] data center; confirm this on the account. The organizers who run our forms can read your answers. JotForm's pages also run JotForm's own scripts, and its privacy policy covers what JotForm itself records.

How long we keep it

We keep form responses for [RETENTION]. After that we delete them from JotForm.

Getting your data deleted

To see or delete what you sent us, email info@midcoastsolidarity.org. Tell us which form you filled out and the name or email you used. We will delete your responses from JotForm.

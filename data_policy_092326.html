Data Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

This page covers what data our website and forms collect, who can see that data, and what eventually happens to that data. We don't have lawyers, so this isn't a legal privacy policy. If something looks wrong, tell us and we'll try to fix it!

This website

This website doesn't run JavaScript or set cookies. You can check for yourself with right-click + Inspect, or with uBlock Origin. That said, GitHub hosts this site, so GitHub's servers see your IP address and the date/time stamps that you visit this page and all of its sub-pages (like any other web host). If that is a problem for you, consider using a reputable VPN (ideally one that is open-source and passes independent security audits).

GitHub (a US company) never sees what you type into our forms, because the forms aren't on this site. They live on JotForm instead.

What we collect

Both of our signup forms run on JotForm, a separate US company. What you type into them goes to JotForm, not to this website.

The Email List and Interest Form asks for:

  • Your name, pronouns, town, and the best way to reach you
  • A phone number or Signal username, but only if you want to attend the virtual book club meetings
  • Which activities and book club meetings you're interested in
  • Skills you can share, a project you need help with, and how you heard about us

The Membership Interest Form asks for:

  • Your name and email
  • Whether you want to become a member, and whether you have time for a vetting interview
  • Which of our events you've been to or plan to go to

The Email List and Interest Form only requires a way to reach you, so you can skip the rest. The Membership Interest Form needs your name and email.

JotForm's pages run JotForm's own scripts, and JotForm records the IP address you send a form from. Our newsletter goes out through Brevo, which tracks opens and link clicks by default.

Where your data lives

Your form answers live on JotForm's servers, and the organizers who run our forms can read them. The email addresses on our newsletter list live with Brevo (a French company).

Deleting your data isn't instant. A deleted form response sits in JotForm's trash for up to 30 days before it's gone for good.

Who sees your data

Our forms and newsletter go through other companies, so those companies see your data too. JotForm's privacy policy covers what JotForm itself records, and Brevo's privacy policy covers what Brevo itself records.

Proton is our email provider. If you email us at info@midcoastsolidarity.org, Proton sees your name and email. It also sees any reply we send you. Your own email provider sees every email we send you too, and that part is out of our hands.

Unsubscribing and downloading/deleting your data

We delete form responses once we've acted on them, or sooner if you ask.

To see what you sent us, get a copy, or have it deleted, email info@midcoastsolidarity.org. Tell us which form you filled out and the name or email you used. We'll delete your responses from JotForm and take you off our Brevo list.

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, since Brevo requires one in every bulk email. You can also go to either company directly. JotForm takes deletion requests through its own request form. If you send a privacy request to Brevo, it passes the request on to us.

What we can and can't prove

This website's code is public on our GitHub, but our forms and newsletter run on JotForm and Brevo. We can't check what those companies actually do with your data, and neither can you. So we're asking you to trust us and them, but nothing we can say or show you is a guarantee. In the end, you still need to make your own calls about your own data.
